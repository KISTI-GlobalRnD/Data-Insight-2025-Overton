2026 Data Update

Quick links: Main Report (Korean PDF) · SI Overview · Methods & Data Validity · Interactive · Appendix · Next Questions · Korean version

Original baseline and 2026 update
  • The Main Report (Korean PDF) is the official baseline organized around the December 2025 publication version.
  • This page reapplies the same analytic frame to Overton 2026-01-30 and OpenAlex 202602 data.
  • It does not replace the official report. Use it as a data-update layer for checking what changes under newer data.

Key summary of the 2026 data update

The newer data still supports the two headline patterns: hub concentration and Korea’s directional asymmetry. The updated values, however, show weaker concentration and a shifted domain mix relative to the original report.

Key item Original report baseline 2026 data update Interpretation
Inbound policy citations to Korean research Top 3 policy sources: 54.8% Top 3 policy sources: 51.1% Concentration in a small set of policy sources remains, but the intensity is slightly lower.
Research cited by Korean policy documents U.S. research 55.7% / Korean research 4.5% U.S. research 48.6% / Korean research 3.8% The U.S. skew remains, but the concentration is weaker.
Domain composition Social Sciences 77.9% / Physical Sciences 9.2% Social Sciences 66.2% / Physical Sciences 18.0% Domain asymmetry remains, but the composition moves toward a more distributed profile.
  • Policy-document citations are still concentrated around a small set of policy sources.
  • Korea should still be interpreted through two separate directions: inbound policy citations to Korean research and research cited by Korean policy documents.
  • The operational frame of tracking repeated citation structure remains valid.

Update scope

  • Overton: 2026-01-30 raw
  • OpenAlex: local 202602 meta/text/topics
  • Fallback: first_author_country and is_oa use remote 202506 supplement values where local OpenAlex 202602 did not contain those fields.
  • Comparison baseline: Overton 2025.02 + OpenAlex 2025.06, the data combination used in the original report.

Data profiling

The updated data also expands the underlying sample.

Item Original report baseline 2026 data update Change
Policy-document-to-paper citation rows 23.37M 28.96M +23.9%
Policy documents citing scholarly papers 1.348M 1.650M +22.4%
Unique cited papers 6.532M 7.425M +13.7%

These shifts should not be read as structural change alone. The newer data reflects both a wider coverage base and changes in the observed sample. With that caveat, the appropriate reading is that hub concentration and Korea’s asymmetry remain, while the concentration strength and domain composition are partly adjusted.

The Korea-related sample also grows.

Korea-related sample Original report baseline 2026 data update Change
Rows citing Korean research 89K 115K +29.0%
Policy documents tagged with South Korea and citing scholarly papers 2,726 25,387 +831.3%
Citation rows tagged with South Korea as a policy source 33K 287K +770.4%

These shifts in Korea-related measures reflect both possible structural change and the much larger observed sample directly connected to Korea. Policy-source tags may not be mutually exclusive, so the increase in rows tagged South Korea widens both the denominator and the scope of Korea-related comparison.

Core changes

Indicator Original report baseline 2026 data update Change
Inbound policy citations to Korean research: top 3 policy-source tags 54.8% 51.1% -3.7 pp
Share of U.S. research in citations from Korean policy documents 55.7% 48.6% -7.1 pp
Share of Korean research in citations from Korean policy documents 4.5% 3.8% -0.8 pp
Share of unique papers cited at least twice 48.1% 51.3% +3.2 pp

The update changes several Korea-related headline values, but it does not overturn the two main messages in the report. Hub concentration remains, and Korea’s asymmetry remains. The updated data mainly softens or shifts the strength of concentration and the domain mix.

Where the larger movements occur

1. Korea’s two directional routes

  • The top-3 share for inbound policy citations to Korean research falls from 54.8% -> 51.1%.
  • The U.S. research share in citations from Korean policy documents falls from 55.7% -> 48.6%.
  • The Korean research share in citations from Korean policy documents falls from 4.5% -> 3.8%.

This does not require rewriting the report’s core sentence, but the Korea section should be read as showing somewhat weaker concentration in the updated data. See the Appendix and Interactive pages for the surrounding diagnostics.

2. Domestic-citation share

  • Korea’s domestic-citation share falls from 4.5% -> 3.8%.
  • The expected domestic-citation share rises from 0.59% -> 0.72%.

In other words, the domestic research share declines while the expected share rises slightly. The gap relative to the expected share is therefore smaller than in the official report. See the Appendix and Interactive pages for the related interpretation checks.

3. Domain demand and supply

Domain Original policy-citation share Updated policy-citation share Original inbound share for Korean research Updated inbound share for Korean research
Social Sciences 77.9% 66.2% 23.7% 29.5%
Health Sciences 9.4% 11.2% 40.9% 34.3%
Physical Sciences 9.2% 18.0% 22.7% 24.1%
Life Sciences 3.5% 4.6% 12.6% 12.1%

The largest movement is the lower Social Sciences share and the higher Physical Sciences share. Korea’s domain-level asymmetry remains, but the composition moves in a more distributed direction than in the official report. See the Appendix and Interactive pages for related checks.

What remains stable

  • Policy-document citations are still concentrated around a small set of policy sources.
  • Korea should still be interpreted through separate inbound and policy-sourcing routes.
  • The repeated-citation monitoring frame remains useful.

The direction of the official report therefore remains intact, but the exact values and domain composition should be rechecked whenever the data are updated.

Interpretation cautions

  • This page applies the same analytic frame as the official report to newer data.
  • The update values are for web comparison and do not automatically replace the official report text.
  • Because local OpenAlex 202602 lacked some first_author_country and is_oa fields, remote 202506 supplement values were used for those fields.
